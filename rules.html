PLAY FAIR

SERVER RULES.

Read before you play. Breaking rules can lead to punishment, including loss of rewards.

01

NO CHEATING

Hacked clients, unfair modifications and combat advantages are not allowed.

02

NO EXPLOITING

Do not abuse bugs, glitches or unintended mechanics.

03

NO DUPING

Duplicating or knowingly distributing exploited items is prohibited.

04

RESPECT PLAYERS

No harassment, threats, hate speech or targeted abuse.

05

NO SCAMMING

Do not intentionally deceive players during trades or deals.

06

KEEP IT APPROPRIATE

No NSFW or inappropriate builds, skins, names or messages.

07

NO IMPERSONATION

Do not pretend to be staff or another player.

08

NO SPAMMING

Avoid chat spam, message flooding and disruptive advertising.

09

NO ADVERTISING

Do not advertise other servers or communities without permission.

10

FOLLOW STAFF

Staff instructions must be followed during moderation situations.

⚠ FINAL NOTICEDisobeying these rules can result in punishment and may lead to the loss of rewards.
